# Privacy Policy for Subscription Tracker

**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

Welcome to Subscription Tracker. Your privacy is critically important to us, and we are committed to safeguarding your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the app, you agree to the data practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide, personalize, and improve our subscription management services, we collect the following types of information:

### Personal & Account Information
* **Google Account Data (Name, Email, and Profile Picture):** Our app uses Google Sign-In for secure and quick authentication. When you choose to log in with your Google account, we automatically receive your basic profile information—specifically your name, email address, and profile picture—directly from Google. We use this to authenticate your identity, create your user profile, and provide cross-device access to your data.

### User-Generated Data
* **Subscription Details:** We securely collect the information you manually input regarding your subscriptions, such as service names, billing cycles, payment dates, and costs. This data is essential to provide the app’s core tracking functionality.

### Automatically Collected Data & Device Information
* **Notification Tokens:** To send you timely reminders and alerts about upcoming renewals or payments, we collect the device tokens required to enable push notifications.
* **Device & Usage Data:** We automatically collect analytical data to ensure the app functions properly. This may include your device model, operating system, IP address, crash reports, and basic usage statistics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to provide a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we use your information to:

* Create, manage, and secure your user account.
* Personalize your experience by displaying your name and Google profile picture within the app.
* Provide the core functionality of tracking, organizing, and managing your subscriptions.
* Deliver push notifications and reminders regarding your upcoming subscription renewals.
* Analyze usage trends to troubleshoot bugs and improve app performance.
* Prevent fraudulent transactions and protect the security of your account.

---

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We prioritize your privacy and **do not sell** your personal data to third parties. We only share your information in the following limited circumstances:

* **Service Providers:** We may share data with trusted third-party vendors that perform services on our behalf, such as secure cloud hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
* **Legal Obligations:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, legal process, or legitimate requests from public authorities to protect the rights, property, and safety of our users.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information (including your Google profile data) and subscription data only for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide our services. 

**How to Delete Your Account and Data:**
In compliance with app store guidelines, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. You can permanently delete your profile data, email, and subscription records through any of the following methods:

1. Navigating to the app settings and selecting the **"Delete Account"** option.
2. Submitting an account deletion request using our dedicated **Google Forms link**.
3. Reaching out to us directly at **hello@westack.ai** with a formal request to delete your account.

Upon requesting deletion, all your personal information, Google profile data, and subscription records will be permanently erased from our active databases.

---

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location and platform, you have the right to:
* **Access, Update, or Delete:** Review, modify, or delete the information in your account at any time.
* **Manage Permissions:** Withdraw consent for push notifications at any time via your mobile device’s system settings.
* **Manage Google Access:** You can revoke Subscription Tracker's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account security settings.

## 6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information and financial tracking data. However, please be aware that no security system is completely impenetrable, and no method of data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Subscription Tracker is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take immediate steps to delete it.
