## 🗑️ Account & Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with modern app store regulations (including Google Play and Apple App Store guidelines), all our applications that utilize user accounts provide seamless, built-in methods for account and data deletion.

Depending on the specific application, you can delete your data by:
1. Using the in-app **"Delete Account"** feature located in the app's settings menu.
2. Submitting a formal deletion request via the designated **Google Forms link** provided in the app's specific privacy policy.
3. Sending a direct email request to our support team at **[hello@westack.ai](mailto:hello@westack.ai)**.

When contacting us via email for data deletion, please include the **app name** in the subject line (e.g., *"Account Deletion Request - Subscription Tracker"*).
