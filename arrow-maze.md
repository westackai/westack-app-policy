# Privacy Policy for Arrow Maze

**Last Updated:** November 17, 2026

Welcome to Arrow Maze, a casual arrow puzzle game published by **WeStack Solutions LLP** ("WeStack", "we", "us", or "our"). Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Arrow Maze on Android or iOS, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading or playing the game, you agree to the data practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Summary

* **No account needed.** Arrow Maze has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.
* **Your progress stays on your device.** Levels, stars, coins, lives, streaks, and settings are saved only on your phone or tablet. They are not sent to our servers.
* **Ads and crash reports.** The game is free and supported by ads from **Google AdMob**. We also use **Firebase Crashlytics** (by Google) to find and fix crashes. These services collect some device and usage data automatically, as described below.
* **We do not sell your personal data.**

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information Stored Only on Your Device
To save your game, the app stores the following data locally on your device:

* **Game Progress:** Current and highest unlocked level, stars and results for each level, coins, score, lives and the life-refill timer, and your daily play streak.
* **Preferences:** Sound, music, and vibration settings, music volume, selected world theme, and whether you have already seen in-game tips.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

### Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services
When the device is connected to the internet, the following services built into the game collect data automatically:

* **Advertising Data (Google AdMob):** Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system, language, app version, and interactions with ads (such as views, clicks, and completed rewarded videos). On supported Android devices, Google may also use the Android Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs (Topics, attribution reporting).
* **Crash & Diagnostic Data (Firebase Crashlytics):** When the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent that includes the error details and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, available memory and disk space, the time of the crash, and a random Crashlytics installation identifier. This identifier is not linked to your name or any account.
* **App Update Checks (Google Play):** On Android, the game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by the Google Play Store app on your device.
* **Fonts (Google Fonts):** The game may download font files from Google's font servers, which receive your IP address as part of the download.

### Information You Choose to Share
* **Share Feature:** When you tap **Share**, the game creates a text message and image of your progress (for example, the level you just cleared) and opens your device's share menu. Only you choose where it goes, and we do not receive a copy.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

* Save your progress and settings so you can continue where you left off.
* Show ads that keep the game free, including optional rewarded ads (for example, for extra hints, lives, or doubled coins), and measure how those ads perform.
* Prevent ad fraud and invalid traffic.
* Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes, bugs, and performance problems.
* Let you know when an updated version of the game is available.

## 6. Your Advertising Choices

* **Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland):** Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.
* **Personalized Ads on Android:** Go to **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads**) to delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads.
* **Personalized Ads on iOS:** Go to **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** to stop apps from requesting to track you.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

* **On-Device Data:** Stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data (**Android:** Settings → Apps → Arrow Maze → Storage → Clear storage). Deleting it resets your progress and cannot be undone.
* **Crash Reports:** Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically.
* **Advertising Data:** Retained by Google according to [Google's retention policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

**How to Request Deletion:**
Because Arrow Maze has no accounts, we do not hold any data that identifies you by name or email. If you would still like us to delete any diagnostic data linked to your installation, email us at **[hello@westack.ai](mailto:hello@westack.ai)** with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request - Arrow Maze"**. We will respond within 30 days.
